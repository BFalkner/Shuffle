Ember Study

A window onto the whole home page, with its campfire where the real one is. Every ember comes from the fire. Scroll the window with the mouse wheel, by dragging, or with the slider, and the embers you see are the ones that would be there.

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the embers are paused. Press Play to run them.

How it works

  • The fire throws out embers at a steady rate. Ember number n is born at a fixed time, and everything about it comes from a random generator seeded by n: its size, heat, lifespan, launch speed, path and flicker.
  • Its height after t seconds is a formula: launched fast, then settling to a slow drift as the hot air's lift fades. Turbulence and a shared wind are smooth random noise, also seeded, so they are erratic but known at any moment.
  • The hot air spreads as a cone, about a tenth as wide as it is high, sideways and toward or away from you. Each ember's own distance comes from that cone, and sets its size, speed on screen, scroll parallax and brightness (inverse square, shared over the area it is drawn on).
  • So for any moment and scroll position the embers in view are known exactly. Each frame checks the embers alive anywhere with a few lines of arithmetic and draws the ones in view. Nothing off screen is simulated.
  • The two density figures are met by working out the fire's output and how long embers live, from 1,500 sample embers, whenever a setting changes.
